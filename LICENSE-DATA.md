## Why CC BY 4.0

Every source below is attribution-only: none is share-alike, none is
non-commercial. A derivative may not drop a requirement its inputs carry, so a
public-domain dedication (CC0) is not available to us; a share-alike licence
(ODbL, CC BY-SA) would add an obligation no source imposes and would make reuse
harder for exactly the researchers and NGOs the project is for. CC BY 4.0 is
therefore both the floor and the fit — and it is what four of the six producers
chose for their own data. Decided 2026-09-21; the terms below were read at source
that day.
