Is there a BUILDING anywhere inside the square? A building means a roofed structure — house, block, shed, warehouse, garage, hotel. Any part of any roof inside the square counts, however small a corner. You are not being asked where its edge falls.

The square is 30 m across. It used to be 10 m, judged at its exact centre — but the median building here is 121 m² against a 100 m² pixel, so almost every point landed on the edge of a roof, and an aerial photo shows a roof several metres from the footprint beneath it. No one can read that reliably. A 30 m square is larger than the disagreement, so the question has an answer.

Why the centre and not the majority: the map was drawn by the same rule. A pixel is marked built when a building footprint covers its centre point, so judging the centre compares like with like. Scoring by how much of the square is filled would measure a different quantity than the map ever claimed.

Why roads do not count: this is measuring the cadastre, the building register, and it contains buildings and nothing else — its "other constructions" file for one municipality is 87 features, every one an open-air swimming pool, with no road anywhere in it. Marking a road as built would score an omission against a map that never claimed roads exist, which measures the definition rather than the accuracy. Road and paving coverage is a different layer, checked separately.

If a greenhouse fills the square, mark unsure and note it — covered agriculture is deliberately its own layer. Use unsure too whenever a leaning roof, a shadow or a mosaic seam means you genuinely cannot tell.

The two photos will not look identical, and should not. Tall roofs lean away from the camera and lean differently in each flight, so buildings appear to shift while the ground does not — check alignment on flat features: kerbs, road markings, path edges. Colour and sun angle can jump across a mosaic seam. And of course the ground may genuinely have changed, which is the thing you are here to judge.

Stop whenever you like — progress is saved in this browser and reopening resumes at the first unanswered point. You can download part-way and score what you have: the estimator is valid at any number of points, it just gives wider intervals. Do look in order to catch problems; do not stop because a number looks good.
